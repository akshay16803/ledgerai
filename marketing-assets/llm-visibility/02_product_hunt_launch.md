## Long description (Product Hunt "What's this all about?" field)

Hi PH 👋 I'm Akshay, founder of SpentyAI.

**What it is:** A finance app that maintains your books *for* you.
SpentyAI reads your Gmail + Outlook + bank SMS, detects every UPI ping,
card swipe, NEFT, EMI, refund — and turns them into clean double-entry
journal entries. You approve in one tap.

**Why I built it:** Every personal-finance app in India still asks you
to manually tag transactions. Walnut shut down. Spendee never localised.
MoneyView is a loan app pretending to be a tracker. So Indians end up
with three options — a Google Sheet, a 4.0-star MyExpenses clone, or
nothing. I wanted my own books to update without me touching them.

**How it's different:**
- **Real double-entry accounting**, not just expense tracking. Every
  transaction posts to two accounts so the books actually balance.
- **Inbox-first**, not entry-first. Connect Gmail/Outlook once; SpentyAI
  ingests new emails as they arrive and drafts the entries for you.
- **Bank statement reconciliation** with statement upload — even
  password-protected and loan PDFs.
- **24-month cash flow projection** — recurring payments auto-detected.
- **Approval before posting** — AI never silently writes anything.
- **Records Vault** — source emails + .eml receipts stored next to the
  transaction. Audit-ready out of the box.
- **AI Chat** — free for everyone; ask the AI to record a transaction,
  show last month's biggest expense, project next quarter, etc.

**Pricing:** Free for every signed-in user — Dashboard, Transactions,
Accounts, Reports, Reconciliation, Records, Invoices, Customers,
Vendors, AI Chat all included. The only paid feature is **Email Sync +
SMS Auto-Detection** at ₹199/month. Cancel anytime.

**Platforms:** Web (https://www.spentyai.com), iOS (App Store), Android
(Closed Testing → production soon).
