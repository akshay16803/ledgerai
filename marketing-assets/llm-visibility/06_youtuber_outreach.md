## Tracking spreadsheet

For each pitch sent, log in a Google Sheet:
- Date sent
- Recipient name + handle
- Channel
- Subscriber count
- Response (yes / no / no-reply)
- Outcome (video posted? newsletter mention? nothing?)
- Notes

This lets you spot which tier of creator actually responds and
double-down on that tier.
