## Description

SpentyAI is the intelligent finance manager that puts AI to work on your money. Whether you're tracking personal expenses, managing a small business, or running a growing enterprise, SpentyAI gives you the clarity and control you need -- all in one app.

SMART EXPENSE & INCOME TRACKING
Track every rupee and dollar across unlimited accounts. Categorize transactions automatically with AI, manage cash, bank, credit card, and digital wallet accounts, and see your complete financial picture at a glance. SpentyAI supports transfers between accounts so your books always balance.

AI-POWERED BANK STATEMENT RECONCILIATION
Import bank statements in CSV or PDF format and let our AI engine parse and match transactions automatically. No more manual data entry -- SpentyAI reads your statements, identifies transactions, and reconciles them with your records in seconds.

AUTOMATIC TRANSACTION DETECTION
Connect your Gmail or Outlook inbox to auto-detect financial transactions from email receipts and notifications. Enable SMS sync to capture transaction alerts from your bank. SpentyAI works silently in the background so nothing slips through the cracks.

PROFESSIONAL INVOICING
Create polished sales invoices with full GST support for Indian businesses -- CGST, SGST, IGST, and HSN codes included. Generate international invoices in any currency. Email invoices directly to clients, track payment status, and manage outstanding receivables effortlessly.

PURCHASE BILL MANAGEMENT
Snap a photo of any bill or receipt and let AI extract the details. SpentyAI parses vendor name, amounts, tax breakdowns, and line items automatically. Keep all your purchase records organized and accessible.

CASH FLOW FORECASTING
See into your financial future with intelligent cash flow projections. SpentyAI analyzes your income and expense patterns to forecast up to 24 months ahead, helping you plan for lean periods and capitalize on growth opportunities.

POWERFUL FINANCIAL REPORTS
Generate detailed reports with category breakdowns, trend charts, and comparative analysis. Track spending patterns, monitor budget performance, and export reports for your accountant or tax filing.

AI CHAT ASSISTANT
Talk to your finances. Ask SpentyAI questions like "How much did I spend on groceries last month?" or "Create an invoice for Client X." The AI assistant understands natural language and can answer questions, create transactions, and surface insights from your data.

CUSTOMER & VENDOR MANAGEMENT
Maintain a complete directory of your customers and vendors. Track aging receivables and payables, view transaction history per contact, and stay on top of who owes you and who you owe.

BUILT FOR INDIA, WORKS GLOBALLY
SpentyAI defaults to INR with full UPI and GST support, making it the ideal choice for Indian freelancers, small businesses, and professionals. Switch to any currency and tax system for international use.

CROSS-PLATFORM SYNC
Your data stays in sync between the SpentyAI web app and iOS app. Start an invoice on your phone, finish it on your laptop. Every transaction, report, and setting is available everywhere you need it.

SUBSCRIPTION
SpentyAI is available via Apple In-App Purchase subscription. Start with a free trial and unlock the full power of AI-driven finance management.

Download SpentyAI today and let AI take the stress out of your finances.
